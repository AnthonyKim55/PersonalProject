About Rebound

Rebound is an MYP Personal Project. It gives basketball players with a sprained ankle a day-by-day rehab plan that matches their injury.

The plans are based on published sports medicine rehab protocols and research reviews, and they use typical recovery times. Every ankle heals at its own pace, so the "How you should feel" checks decide when to move on, not the calendar.

How it works

  1. Pick your sprain. Choose the injured area and the grade.
  2. Tell us when it happened. Your plan starts at the block that matches your recovery day.
  3. Follow the blocks. Move on only when you match the next block's "How you should feel" list.

Rebound does not replace a doctor, physiotherapist, or athletic trainer. Grade III sprains always need a professional.
