About this site

Back on Court is an MYP Personal Project. The plans are based on published sports medicine rehab protocols and research reviews, and they use typical recovery times. Every ankle heals at its own pace, so the "How you should feel" checks decide when to move on, not the calendar. This site does not replace a doctor, physiotherapist, or athletic trainer.
